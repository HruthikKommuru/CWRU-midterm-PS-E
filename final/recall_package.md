# CareWell Pharmacy Product Recall — Team Draft

**Classroom exercise only. Prepared for team review; not sent to customers or the Board and not published.**

## Customer notice

### Recall: ClearBreath Cough Syrup, batch 4471

CareWell Pharmacy is recalling **ClearBreath Cough Syrup, 120 mL bottles, batch 4471**. Testing found an unintended microbial contaminant in this batch. The affected bottles expire **February 28, 2029**. Check the batch number on your bottle. **Batch 4470 is not part of this recall.**

Taking batch 4471 may cause nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, or fever. Children under 12 are at higher risk.

**What to do**

- Stop using batch 4471 and keep the bottle away from children.
- Return it to any operating CareWell Pharmacy for a full refund. Bring the bottle if available; no receipt is required. You may return it at a different store from where you bought it. CareWell will refund the original payment method where possible, or offer cash otherwise.
- If you feel unwell after taking it, contact a healthcare professional promptly. Severe symptoms require urgent medical help.

Harbor Annex closed on March 31, 2027. If you bought the product there, return it to another operating CareWell store.

**Recall hotline: 1-800-555-0199**, daily **8:00 AM–8:00 PM Eastern**, beginning April 16, 2027.

CareWell Pharmacy, 180 Meridian Avenue, Northbridge, Ohio 44000, submits this recall report. The responsible contact is Lena Ortiz, Quality Manager, `quality@carewell.example`.

The manufacturer is Cedar Health Products, 45 Industry Road, Northbridge, Ohio 44000. The product is ClearBreath Cough Syrup, an oral liquid in 120 mL bottles. The recalled batch is **4471**, expiring **February 28, 2029**. It was manufactured February 8, 2027, bottled February 9, and shipped February 15. The laboratory report was released to CareWell on April 16, 2027, at 9:00 AM Eastern.

Retained samples from batch 4471 contained an unintended microbial contaminant and failed the release specification. Exposure may cause nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, or fever; children under 12 are at higher risk. Batch 4470 passed release and retained-sample testing and is outside this recall.

### Sales, affected locations, and stock control

CareWell received 1,200 bottles of batch 4471. At report time, 311 were held at the warehouse and 889 had been distributed. Store stock control reported 216 bottles on shelves or in back rooms; no customer returns had been recorded by 9:00 AM April 16. Staff must quarantine remaining stock immediately, log all returns, quarantine returned goods for quality-team collection, and not resell recalled product.

Sales records cover February 16 through April 15, 2027. Rows explicitly coded as batch 4471 total **609 bottles**. The S08 row lists 64 bottles as batch **4417**, although the batch report records 75 bottles of 4471 delivered to S08 and 11 on hand. If that batch code is confirmed as a transcription error, those 64 units bring the sales reconciliation to **673** (889 distributed minus 216 on hand). Until the transaction record is confirmed, 673 is a reconciliation figure, not a verified sales total; do not report the 64 units as confirmed 4471 sales. The batch report says no ClearBreath batches other than 4470 and 4471 were supplied during January–April. S05's 81 recorded sales are batch 4470 and are excluded from this recall.

The affected batch was distributed to these locations. S01–S04 and S06–S12 are currently operating stores. S13 received and historically sold the batch but closed March 31, 2027; its remaining stock went to the central warehouse. S13 is not a customer return location. Customers may return recalled product to any operating CareWell store, including an operating store that did not sell batch 4471.

### Customer instructions and communication

Customers should stop using the product, keep it away from children, and return it to any operating CareWell store for a full refund. A receipt is not required; customers should bring the bottle if available. Refund the original payment method where possible, otherwise cash. Anyone who feels unwell after taking the product should contact a healthcare professional promptly; severe symptoms require urgent medical help.

The recall hotline is **1-800-555-0199**, staffed daily **8:00 AM–8:00 PM Eastern**, beginning April 16, 2027. Store teams will keep a printed recall notice at each operating store counter. The supplied records do not specify an additional customer-communication channel or release schedule; confirm any broader plan before an actual submission.
