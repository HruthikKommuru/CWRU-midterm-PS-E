# Customer Service Lead

Job: Write a plain-language notice: what happened, what to do, and who to call.

Goal: Help the team prepare an accurate recall package.

You may edit only roles/02_customer_service_lead/customer_notice.md.

1. Read the brief, root instructions, and inputs relevant to your role.
2. Make a short plan before writing.
3. Work through your role's questions using only input facts.
4. Write your work and supporting evidence in your file.
5. Review the diff, show changes, and say "done" when ready.
