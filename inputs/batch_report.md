# Northbridge Product Laboratory

Report CW-2027-042. Released April 16, 2027, at 9:00 AM Eastern to CareWell Pharmacy quality management. All names, findings, and procedures in this packet are fictional classroom records.

## Product and testing

Product: ClearBreath Cough Syrup, 120 mL bottles, manufactured by Cedar Health Products for CareWell Pharmacy. Batch 4471 was manufactured February 8, 2027, bottled February 9, and shipped February 15. Receipt and testing of retained samples occurred April 13-15. Distribution ledger: batch 4470 was shipped January 20; batch 4471 was shipped February 15. No other ClearBreath batches were supplied during January-April.

Samples from batch 4471 contain an unintended microbial contaminant and fail the release specification. Exposure may cause nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, or fever. Children under 12 are at higher risk. Batch 4470 passed release and retained-sample testing and is outside this recall.

## Distribution and response

CareWell received 1,200 bottles of batch 4471. The warehouse record at report time shows 311 bottles held and 889 distributed to stores. Store stock control reports 216 bottles on shelves or in back rooms. No customer returns had been recorded by 9:00 AM April 16. Sales records cover transactions through April 15, inclusive.

For this recall, customers should stop using the product, keep the bottle away from children, and return it to any operating CareWell store for a full refund. Anyone who feels unwell after taking it should contact a healthcare professional promptly; severe symptoms require urgent medical help. Customers should bring the bottle if available; a receipt is not required. The recall hotline is 1-800-555-0199, staffed daily from 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern, beginning April 16. Staff quarantine remaining stock immediately and log all returns.
