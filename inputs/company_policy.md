# CareWell Pharmacy service manual

Edition January 2027. CareWell operates 12 pharmacies serving the Northbridge area. Customer service processes full refunds for recalled products at any operating branch, with or without a receipt. Return the purchase price to the original payment method where possible; otherwise offer a cash refund. Record product, quantity, date, and store in the returns log. Quarantine returned goods for collection by the quality team. Do not resell recalled goods.

Customer hotline: 1-800-555-0119, daily 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern. Store teams keep a printed copy of each recall notice at the counter. Customers may return products at any operating store rather than the store of purchase.
